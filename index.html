Multi-Layered Decoy (Wielowarstwowa Iluzja)

Multi-Layered Decoy

EXO doesn't limit you to one fake code. Create a grid of fake identities. The Main PIN opens real data. A "Decoy PIN" opens a fake vault (useful in forced unlock scenarios). An attacker will never know how many layers your fortress has.
