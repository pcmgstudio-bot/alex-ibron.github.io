PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: January 2025

Zero-Data Architecture

Ibron Industries (including apps like IbronEXO and IbronCLOCK) operates on a strict Zero-Data policy. We believe your data belongs to you.

  • No Data Collection: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, location data, or usage analytics.
  • 100% Offline: Our applications function completely independently of internet servers. All your notes, alarms, and settings are saved locally in the encrypted storage of your device via AsyncStorage.
  • Third Parties: Since we do not collect any data, we have absolutely nothing to share with or sell to third-party services.
  • Permissions: Any permissions requested (such as "Display over other apps" in IbronCLOCK) are used solely to trigger local system functions (like waking up the screen).
